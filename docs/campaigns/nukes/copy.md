- **Card 2: Crisis of Leadership** — "Our leaders are increasingly unpredictable. Betting civilization on the emotional restraint of a handful of politicians is a gamble we must end."
- **Card 3: Breaking the Proliferation Trap** — "More states are looking to build the bomb, believing it brings security. We need a global voice proving it only brings a target."
- **Card 4: Multiple Flashpoints** — "Cold War treaties have expired. Regional conflicts are multiplying. The margin of error has evaporated."
- **Card 5: Lowered Tech Barriers** — "Nukes are easier and cheaper to build today than ever before. If we don't build a cultural taboo now, anyone will be able to make one."
