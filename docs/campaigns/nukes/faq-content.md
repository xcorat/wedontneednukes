# [DRAFT] FAQ Content: Nukes Campaign

This document contains the initial FAQ wiki content for the nuclear disarmament campaign. It is structured as topics linked to specific pledge steps.

## Topics

### Why pledge against nuclear weapons?
Taking a pledge is a public statement of values. It aggregates individual voices into a powerful collective message that can influence policy, corporate behavior, and global norms. It shows leaders that there is a broad, uncompromising base demanding disarmament.

### Why now? What makes today different from the past?
The nuclear threat is no longer a frozen Cold War memory. Five converging dynamics make action urgent today:
1. **AI Integration in Military Decision Loops**: Militaries are racing to plug AI into early-warning and command structures. Autonomous systems can hallucinate, suffer from data poisoning, and cannot be engineered with 100% resilience against catastrophic false alarms.
2. **Erosion of Political Trust & Institutional Guardrails**: Trust in global political leaders and institutional checks is at historical lows. Entrusting human survival to the short-term emotional impulses of fallible leaders in unstable political climates is an untenable risk.
3. **The Proliferation Cascade**: Observing modern conflicts, non-nuclear states are dangerously concluding that acquiring nuclear weapons is essential for security. We must actively break this myth: nukes do not protect nations, they paint targets on them and trigger arms races.
4. **Multiplying Hotspots & Collapsed Treaties**: Multiple global flashpoints (Eastern Europe, Middle East, East Asia) exist simultaneously, while Cold War treaty guardrails (INF, Open Skies, bilateral inspection accords) have almost entirely dissolved.
5. **Lowered Technical Barriers**: Modern precision machining, computational modeling, and dual-use tech make designing and enriching warhead components significantly easier and faster than decades ago. If we don't build a strong cultural taboo now, weaponization will steadily spread.

### What does "not supporting expansion" mean practically?
It means refusing to contribute your labor, money, or vocal support to industries, politicians, or policies that aim to build new nuclear weapons or modernize existing arsenals at the expense of disarmament treaties.

### What are market-based opposition strategies?
Market-based strategies involve using your purchasing and investment power. This includes divesting from funds that hold shares in nuclear weapons manufacturers, supporting ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) funds, and boycotting companies involved in the nuclear supply chain.

### What political actions can I take?
You can vote for candidates who explicitly support nuclear disarmament, sign petitions, contact your local representatives, support NGOs like ICAN (International Campaign to Abolish Nuclear Weapons), and participate in peaceful protests.

### How is my data used? (privacy guarantee)
Your privacy is our priority. We collect only what is necessary to authenticate your pledge and prevent spam. We do not sell your data, and your individual contact information is kept strictly confidential. The platform aggregates pledge statistics for public display.

### What happens after I pledge?
After you pledge, your stance is recorded and added to the global count. You will be directed to action pages tailored to your commitment level, where you can find resources, share your pledge, and connect with ongoing campaigns.

### Can I change my pledge?
Yes. As you learn more or your circumstances change, you can log in to your account and update your commitment level to reflect your current stance.
