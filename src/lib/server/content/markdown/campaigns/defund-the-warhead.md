**Defund the Warhead** encompasses grassroots and institutional divestment campaigns organized across Europe and North America by PAX, ICAN partner organizations, and student coalitions.

The campaign directly challenges the economic incentives underpinning the multi-trillion-dollar nuclear modernization programs currently being pursued by nuclear-armed states.

## Target Sectors

1. **University Endowments**: Demanding that university boards divest multi-million-dollar investment portfolios from major defense contractors producing nuclear delivery systems.
2. **Municipal & Public Pension Funds**: Organizing public sector employees, teachers, and municipal workers to exclude nuclear warhead manufacturers from their retirement systems.
3. **Retail Banking**: Educating ordinary depositors on how their everyday savings accounts are deployed by investment banks to underwrite corporate bonds for weapons corporations.

## Tactics & Tools

- Model divestment resolutions tailored for university senates and city councils.
- Shareholder activism frameworks enabling ethical investors to introduce binding proxy resolutions at annual general meetings (AGMs).
- Coordinated boycott actions aligning with Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) standards to classify nuclear weapon manufacturing as a non-compliant activity.
