The **Emerging Voices Network (EVN)** is a global digital initiative established by the British American Security Information Council (BASIC). It provides early-career professionals, postgraduate scholars, and underrepresented voices with direct entry points into nuclear policy, arms control, and disarmament strategy.

## Breaking Nuclear Orthodoxy

Nuclear deterrence discussions have long been dominated by a narrow demographic of defense strategists and retired military officials from nuclear-armed states. EVN works to de-center these perspectives by:
- Engaging researchers from the Global South, indigenous communities affected by nuclear testing, women, and non-binary policy thinkers.
- Rethinking deterrence theories in light of climate disruption, systemic racial inequalities, and cyber vulnerabilities.

## Network Outputs

- **Policy Briefs**: Collaborative working groups draft annual policy recommendations presented ahead of UN Non-Proliferation Treaty (NPT) and TPNW diplomatic conferences.
- **Global Consultations**: Digital roundtables connecting young analysts from over 40 countries directly with seasoned ambassadors and negotiators.
- **Mentorship Programs**: Pairing emerging scholars with established leaders in disarmament diplomacy and peace research.
