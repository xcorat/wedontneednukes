**Global Zero** is an international civil society movement launched in Paris in December 2008, bringing together more than 300 political, military, and diplomatic leaders alongside hundreds of thousands of citizen activists worldwide.

The campaign's core thesis is that non-proliferation without total disarmament is unstable, and that complete, verified elimination is the only reliable defense against nuclear catastrophe, accidental launch, or nuclear terrorism.

## The Global Zero Action Plan

Global Zero developed a four-phase technical roadmap for disarmament:
1. **Bilateral Deep Cuts**: Substantial reductions in deployed and tactical warheads between the United States and Russia.
2. **Multilateral Arms Negotiations**: Bringing China, France, the UK, India, Pakistan, and others into legally binding reduction frameworks.
3. **Verified Drawdown**: Phased dismantlement under international on-site inspections and satellite monitoring.
4. **Permanent Prohibition**: Implementation of international treaties outlawing the development, possession, or deployment of nuclear warheads.

## Key Campaigns

- **Fail-Safe Campaign**: Demanding an end to sole-launch authority and the removal of ballistic missiles from hair-trigger alert status.
- **No First Use Policy**: Urging nuclear-armed states to formally adopt legally binding pledges never to be the first to initiate nuclear conflict.
