The **Mayors for Peace "VOICE" Initiative** is the cornerstone civic action framework of the *Mayors for Peace* 2021–2025 and 2025–2029 Action Plans. It harnesses municipal leadership across more than 8,500 member cities in 166 countries to accelerate the global realization of a nuclear-weapon-free world.

## The VOICE Acronym

- **V – Voices of Hibakusha**: Recording, translating, and broadcasting firsthand witness testimonies from survivors of Hiroshima and Nagasaki before living memory passes.
- **O – Outreach for TPNW**: Lobbying national governments and organizing regional civic campaigns to universalize accession to the Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons.
- **I – International Advocacy**: Representing municipal authorities at the UN General Assembly, NPT Review Conferences, and TPNW Meetings of States Parties.
- **C – Citizen Action**: Facilitating global petition campaigns, peace poster exhibitions, and youth exchange programs in towns and cities worldwide.
- **E – Encouraging Transformation**: Shifting community investments and national defense spending toward human security, pandemic preparedness, and climate resilience.

## Municipal Impact

By working through local government leaders who are directly responsible for the safety of urban populations, the VOICE Initiative demonstrates that cities are the primary targets of nuclear weapons and therefore have a moral obligation to demand their complete elimination.
