**International Student / Young Pugwash (ISYP)** is a global network of university students, young scientists, and early-career policy researchers inspired by the Russell-Einstein Manifesto and the Nobel Peace Prize-winning Pugwash Conferences on Science and World Affairs.

ISYP bridges the gap between technical disciplines (physics, computer science, engineering) and international relations to train the next generation of scientific diplomats and arms control specialists.

## Activities & Programs

- **University Conferences**: Annual global workshops examining technical verification, outer space militarization, and AI integration into nuclear command-and-control systems.
- **Cross-Disciplinary Working Groups**: Bringing together young engineers and diplomats to formulate actionable policy briefs on emerging dual-use technologies.
- **National Chapters**: Autonomous student groups active on university campuses in the UK, United States, Japan, India, Pakistan, Germany, and Russia.

## Key Themes

ISYP emphasizes the special ethical responsibility of scientists whose research can be repurposed for weapons of mass destruction, promoting science as a force for peace, diplomacy, and global risk reduction.
