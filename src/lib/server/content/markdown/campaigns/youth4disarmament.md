**#Youth4Disarmament** is an initiative established by the United Nations Office for Disarmament Affairs (UNODA) following UN General Assembly Resolution 74/64, recognizing young people as essential agents of change in multilateral disarmament negotiations.

The platform provides youth aged 12 to 30 with learning resources, creative workshops, mentorship programs, and direct pathways into UN disarmament meetings and diplomatic conferences.

## Core Initiatives

- **Disarmament Education**: Interactive online courses covering weapons of mass destruction, conventional arms control, and outer space security.
- **Youth Champions for Disarmament**: Flagship training fellowship that brings young leaders from around the world to Geneva, Hiroshima, and Nagasaki to engage with diplomats and atomic bomb survivors.
- **Creative & Arts Contests**: Utilizing visual arts, writing, and digital media to communicate the urgency of nuclear abolition beyond traditional diplomatic circles.

## Why Youth Engagement Matters

Disarmament discussions historically occurred behind closed doors among state diplomats and military strategists. #Youth4Disarmament bridges grassroots youth enthusiasm with high-level multilateral forums, making disarmament an intersectional issue tied closely to climate justice, public health, and human rights.
