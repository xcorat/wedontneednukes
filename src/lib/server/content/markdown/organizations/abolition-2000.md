**Abolition 2000** is an international civil society network founded during the 1995 Non-Proliferation Treaty (NPT) Review and Extension Conference. It brings together hundreds of non-governmental organizations, peace groups, faith institutions, and policy institutes across more than 90 countries.

## The Abolition 2000 Founding Statement

The network’s charter declares that nuclear weapons are intrinsically immoral, environmentally destructive, and illegal under international law. It demands a binding **Nuclear Weapons Convention** setting out a clear, verifiable timetable for the global elimination of all nuclear arsenals.

## Specialized Working Groups

Abolition 2000 coordinates numerous expert civil society working groups:
- **Missile Defense & Space Weaponization**: Analyzing the destabilizing impacts of anti-ballistic missile systems and outer space militarization.
- **Economic Dimensions of Disarmament**: Auditing national military expenditures and redirecting nuclear weapons budgets toward sustainable development and planetary climate protection.
- **Youth & Disarmament Working Group**: Fostering intergenerational leadership in civil society mobilization.
