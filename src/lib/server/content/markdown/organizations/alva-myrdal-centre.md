The **Alva Myrdal Centre for Nuclear Disarmament (AMC)** was established in 2021 at Uppsala University with direct support from the Swedish Government. Named in honor of Alva Myrdal—Swedish diplomat, pioneer of multilateral disarmament negotiations, and 1982 Nobel Peace Prize laureate—the Centre unites academic research, policy engagement, and graduate education.

## Interdisciplinary Working Groups

AMC brings together scholars across peace and conflict research, international public law, political science, and nuclear physics:
- **Disarmament Verification**: Developing radiation-detection methods and physical models to verify that declared nuclear warheads are dismantled irreversibly without revealing classified construction designs.
- **International Law & Norms**: Evaluating compliance mechanisms between the Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of Nuclear Weapons (NPT) and the Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons (TPNW).
- **Early-Career Training**: Equipping the next generation of civil servants, researchers, and NGO leaders with technical and diplomatic skills.
