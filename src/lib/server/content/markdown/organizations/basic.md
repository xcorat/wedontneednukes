The **British American Security Information Council (BASIC)** is an independent international policy institute and think-tank based in London, with historical offices in Washington D.C. Established in 1987, BASIC specializes in nuclear risk reduction, multilateral disarmament diplomacy, and international security policy.

## Strategic Research & Dialogue

- **Nuclear Responsibilities Program**: A diplomatic dialogue framework that moves beyond traditional deterrence posturing by engaging nuclear-armed states on their mutual responsibilities under international law and the NPT.
- **Stepping Stones & Risk Reduction**: Formulating actionable confidence-building measures to prevent accidental escalations between NATO and Russia, and between regional nuclear rivals in South and East Asia.
- **[Emerging Voices Network (EVN)](https://basicint.org/emerging-voices-network/)**: Empowering early-career researchers, women, and Global South scholars to challenge status-quo strategic doctrine.
