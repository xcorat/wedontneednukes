**International Physicians for the Prevention of Nuclear War (IPPNW)** is a non-partisan global federation of medical doctors, public health professionals, and medical students across more than 60 countries.

Founded at the height of the Cold War in 1980 by American cardiologist Bernard Lown and Soviet cardiologist Evgeny Chazov, IPPNW proved that medical and scientific collaboration could transcend geopolitical hostilities to address the greatest singular threat to global public health. IPPNW was awarded the **1985 Nobel Peace Prize** for its groundbreaking public education on the catastrophic medical consequences of nuclear war.

## Founding ICAN & The Humanitarian Movement

In 2005, IPPNW proposed the creation of a dedicated global campaign to ban nuclear weapons, officially launching the International Campaign to Abolish Nuclear Weapons (ICAN) in 2007. IPPNW continues to serve on the International Steering Group of ICAN.

## Scientific & Medical Research

IPPNW researchers, in collaboration with climate scientists like Alan Robock, pioneered modern scientific studies on **Nuclear Famine**:
- Demonstrating that even a regional nuclear exchange (e.g., using less than 1% of the global nuclear arsenal) would loft millions of tons of black carbon into the stratosphere, causing catastrophic global cooling, widespread agricultural collapse, and putting over 2 billion people at risk of starvation.
- Clarifying that the international medical community possesses no effective treatment or response capability in the aftermath of a nuclear detonation.
