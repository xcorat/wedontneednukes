**Mayors for Peace** is an international municipal non-governmental organization established in 1982 by Takeshi Araki, the then-Mayor of Hiroshima, together with the Mayor of Nagasaki. Its mission is to awaken citizen consciousness to the complete abolition of nuclear weapons and to achieve lasting world peace by addressing inter-related urban challenges such as starvation, poverty, refugee crises, and human rights abuses.

Today, Mayors for Peace connects approximately **8,500 member cities across 166 countries and territories**, representing over one billion citizens worldwide.

## Structural Leadership & Strategy

The organization is chaired by the Mayor of Hiroshima, with the Mayor of Nagasaki serving as Vice President. It organizes global mayoral delegations that participate directly in United Nations disarmament forums, NPT Review Conferences, and Meetings of States Parties to the Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons (TPNW).

## Key Programs

- **VOICE Initiative (Action Plan 2025–2029)**: Elevating hibakusha testimonies, conducting outreach for TPNW universalization, and mobilizing citizens through petition drives.
- **Peace Education & Poster Exhibitions**: Supplying high schools, libraries, and city halls across member municipalities with educational photo exhibitions depicting the reality of the atomic bombings.
- **Youth Exchange & Peace Ambassadors**: Sponsoring university and high school students to visit Hiroshima and Nagasaki for immersive peace study workshops.
