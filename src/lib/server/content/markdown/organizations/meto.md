The **Middle East Treaty Organization (METO)** is an independent civil society initiative bringing together regional diplomats, academics, activists, and technical experts dedicated to ridding the Middle East of nuclear weapons and all other weapons of mass destruction (chemical, biological, and radiological).

## The Middle East WMDFZ Goal

Since 1974, the United Nations General Assembly has endorsed the concept of a **Middle East Weapons of Mass Destruction-Free Zone (ME-WMDFZ)**, formalized as a binding resolution at the 1995 NPT Review and Extension Conference. Decades of regional hostility and state impasse, however, stalled progress.

## METO's Grassroots & Track-II Strategy

METO circumvents inter-governmental inertia by taking a bottom-up, collaborative approach:
- **Draft Treaty Project**: Collaboratively authoring a complete, clause-by-clause model treaty for a Middle East WMDFZ with input from experts across Israel, Iran, Arab states, and international verification organizations.
- **Education & Training**: Running educational workshops and digital academies for regional university students, building cross-cultural civil society networks committed to human security over mutual destruction.
- **Confidence-Building Measures**: Promoting small, achievable stepping stones, such as regional public health data sharing and environmental monitoring, to establish baseline diplomatic trust.
