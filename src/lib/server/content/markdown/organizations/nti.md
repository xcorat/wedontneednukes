The **Nuclear Threat Initiative (NTI)** is a non-partisan, non-profit global security organization co-founded in 2001 by former US Senator Sam Nunn and philanthropist Ted Turner. NTI focuses on reducing nuclear, biological, and emerging technological threats imperiling humanity.

## Groundbreaking Technical Verification & Policy

NTI works with governments, international institutions, and scientific laboratories to create operational, technical models for threat reduction:
- **NTI Nuclear Security Index**: A recognized global benchmark tracking the security and theft-protection standards of weapons-usable nuclear materials (highly enriched uranium and plutonium) across dozens of countries.
- **Verification Innovation**: Partnering with international national laboratories to develop zero-knowledge verification protocols, radiation portal monitors, and remote cryptographic sensors that allow inspectors to confirm warhead dismantlement without revealing classified warhead design secrets.
- **Fail-Safe & Early Warning Risk Reduction**: Working with cyber experts to audit the vulnerability of nuclear early warning satellites, command centers, and communication relays against sophisticated cyber attacks and AI-driven deepfake deception.
