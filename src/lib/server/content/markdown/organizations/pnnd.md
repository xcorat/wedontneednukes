**Parliamentarians for Nuclear Non-proliferation and Disarmament (PNND)** is a non-partisan global network of legislators from regional, national, and international parliaments. Operating across party lines in more than 90 countries, PNND provides lawmakers with information, analysis, and legislative drafting tools to advance nuclear risk reduction and total disarmament.

## Key Legislative Actions

- **Budgetary Scrutiny**: Questioning multi-billion-dollar military modernization budgets and introducing amendments to redirect public funds toward healthcare, climate transition, and sustainable development.
- **Nuclear-Weapon-Free Zones (NWFZs)**: Supporting parliamentary ratification of regional treaties declaring entire continents and regions permanently free of nuclear weapons.
- **Inter-Parliamentary Resolutions**: Drafting and passing disarmament resolutions in bodies such as the Inter-Parliamentary Union (IPU), the OSCE Parliamentary Assembly, and the European Parliament.
