The **United Nations Institute for Disarmament Research (UNIDIR)** is an autonomous, voluntarily funded research institute established within the United Nations framework in 1980. Based at the Palais des Nations in Geneva, UNIDIR conducts independent, evidence-based research on current and future security challenges.

## Independent Multilateral Research

UNIDIR assists the international community—including UN member states, the Conference on Disarmament, and the UN General Assembly—by generating rigorous data and technical assessments:
- **Nuclear Risk Reduction**: Identifying cognitive biases, communication failures, and doctrinal ambiguities that could trigger inadvertent nuclear escalation between great powers.
- **Verification Technologies**: Evaluating forensic tools, satellite telemetry analysis, and open-source intelligence monitoring for future multilateral disarmament treaties.
- **AI & Outer Space Security**: Researching the intersection of algorithmic autonomy, uncrewed systems, hypersonic delivery platforms, and space-based early-warning satellites.
