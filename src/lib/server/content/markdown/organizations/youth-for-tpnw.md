**Youth for TPNW** is a transnational network run by and for young people aged 18 to 30. Launched in the wake of the entry into force of the UN Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons (TPNW), the organization focuses on youth mobilization, centering the lived experiences of affected frontline communities, and bridging disarmament advocacy with intersectional climate and racial justice movements.

## Core Pillars

1. **Centering Affected Communities**: Prioritizing the voices of indigenous peoples in the Pacific, Kazakhstan, North America, and Australia whose lands, waters, and bodies were poisoned by atmospheric and underground nuclear weapons tests.
2. **Diplomatic Advocacy**: Organizing youth delegations to the UN Meetings of States Parties (1MSP, 2MSP, 3MSP), directly questioning state delegates and introducing working papers on environmental remediation and victim assistance (Articles 6 & 7 of the TPNW).
3. **Decolonial & Intersectional Education**: Reframing nuclear weapons not as abstract deterrents, but as tools of colonial exploitation and environmental violence.
