## Anonymous votes

If you haven't signed in, your vote is keyed to your `anon_id` cookie.
Returning to the site and voting again will override the prior choice.
Clearing cookies starts you fresh.

## Signed-in pledges

Once you sign in (or claim your anonymous vote), your pledge becomes
attached to your account:

- Update your answer from your dashboard.
- Change or cancel commitment levels at any time.
- There is no escalation workflow — changes apply instantly.

## What is preserved

Previous versions are not retained publicly; only your current stance
is on display. Internal changelog records exist for audit purposes and
are not exposed.
