## What counts as passive opposition

Passive opposition means you don't actively organize or confront — but
you consistently choose not to enable expansion. Concretely:

- **Divestment checks** — avoid funds, pensions, or banks materially
  financing nuclear-weapon programs.
- **Awareness** — read, share, and discuss without confrontation.
- **Steady voting** — vote for candidates and measures that limit
  expansion, even when not the headline issue.

## What passive does not mean

It does not mean silence. Refusing material support is the smallest
unit of dissent whose effect compounds across millions of people.
