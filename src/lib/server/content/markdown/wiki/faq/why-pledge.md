## Public commitments shift norms

Private opinions are easy to dismiss. A named pledge — visible,
countable, durable — changes how policymakers, journalists, and
neighbors perceive where the public stands.

## Numbers matter for mobilization

We are building a live ledger of people who oppose nuclear weapons.
That figure is cited, organized around, and used to drive the next
campaign step.

## The pledge is reversible

You can change or withdraw your stance at any time. The platform
records your current position and updates on request. There is no
contract, no financial commitment, and no social penalty for changing
your mind.
