## What We Do

**We Don't Need Nukes** is an independent, non-partisan initiative measuring public consensus on one fundamental question: **does human civilization need nuclear weapons?**

Diplomatic treaties frequently stall, and military doctrines increasingly integrate automated and AI-driven command systems. Our premise is simple: real policy change requires demonstrable, public consensus from everyday citizens first.

## How It Works

1. **Clear Stance**: Every visitor records their perspective—whether they agree, disagree, or remain undecided.
2. **Commitment Options**: Participants can select practical actions they are willing to take, such as personal, political, or market-based choices.
3. **Transparent Counting**: We aggregate stances to measure consensus without selling user data, tracking across sites, or serving ads.

## Privacy & Open Source

- **Minimal Data**: Anonymous voting is supported. If you sign in, we only store the credentials required for authentication and vote deduplication.
- **No Monetization**: We never sell user data or run third-party advertising trackers.
- **Open Platform**: Built openly so community members can audit and verify how consensus is gathered.
