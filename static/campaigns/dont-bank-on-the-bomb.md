**Don’t Bank on the Bomb** is the premier global research and civil action campaign documenting commercial and investment banks, pension funds, and asset managers financing corporations involved in the production and modernization of nuclear weapons.

Operated jointly by PAX (the Dutch peace organization) and the International Campaign to Abolish Nuclear Weapons (ICAN), the campaign produces annual reports identifying which financial institutions maintain nuclear-free policies (the *Hall of Fame*) and which continue to fund nuclear contractors (the *Hall of Shame*).

## Key Objectives

1. **Financial Transparency**: Provide publicly accessible data detailing billions of dollars channeled into private aerospace and defense contractors manufacturing nuclear warheads and delivery systems.
2. **Institutional Divestment**: Partner with universities, municipal pension funds, faith-based investors, and major asset managers to adopt strict exclusionary policies against nuclear weapons.
3. **Consumer & Citizen Action**: Empower account holders to contact their banking institutions, demand ethical screening, or switch to financial institutions that refuse to profit from existential weapons of mass destruction.

## Impact & Achievements

- Over 110 major financial institutions globally have implemented strict policies forbidding or severely restricting investments in nuclear weapons producers.
- Following the entry into force of the UN Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons (TPNW), major global pension funds, including Norwegian and Dutch funds, have expanded their divestment criteria to include nuclear weapons manufacturers.

- Send a direct message to your institution's board asking them to adopt the ICAN nuclear weapons divestment standard.
