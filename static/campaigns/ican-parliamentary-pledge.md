The **ICAN Parliamentary Pledge** is a cross-party legislative commitment through which elected members of parliament, congress, and national assemblies around the world publicly commit to advancing their government's accession to the Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons (TPNW).

## The Pledge Text

Signatory parliamentarians sign the following commitment:
> *"We, the undersigned parliamentarians, warmly welcome the adoption of the UN Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons, which represents a significant step towards the realization of a nuclear-weapon-free world. We pledge to work for the signature and ratification of this landmark treaty by our respective countries, as we consider the abolition of nuclear weapons to be a global public good of the highest order and an essential step to promote the security and well-being of all peoples."*

## Global Scope

- Signed by thousands of current and former national parliamentarians across Europe, the Americas, Africa, and Asia-Pacific.
- Especially critical in "nuclear-umbrella" and NATO member states where central executive branches remain tied to nuclear sharing policies, but legislative members actively debate and oppose nuclear dependency.

## Engaging Your Representatives

Citizens can find whether their local representatives have signed the pledge by querying the searchable database at [pledge.icanw.org](https://pledge.icanw.org) and sending automated or custom letters encouraging non-signatories to join.
