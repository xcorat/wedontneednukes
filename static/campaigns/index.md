The global movement to eliminate nuclear weapons does not operate solely in diplomatic chambers—it relies on citizen-powered campaigns that build public pressure, demand financial divestment, and organize municipal and legislative commitments worldwide.

Below is a curated directory of active campaigns, public initiatives, and collegiate movements working in coordination with international frameworks like the [Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons (TPNW)](https://www.icanw.org/the_treaty).

---

## Featured Global Campaigns

* **[Don’t Bank on the Bomb](https://www.dontbankonthebomb.com)**
  A global divestment campaign coordinated with ICAN and PAX that tracks global financial institutions investing in companies producing nuclear weapons. The campaign equips citizens, pension holders, and financial institutions with research to divest capital from the nuclear weapons complex.

* **[#Youth4Disarmament](https://youth4disarmament.org)**
  An initiative launched under the United Nations Office for Disarmament Affairs (UNODA) aimed at educating, equipping, and empowering younger generations (ages 12–30) to participate meaningfully in disarmament discourse, arts, and diplomatic forums.

* **[Mayors for Peace "VOICE" Initiative (Action Plan 2025–2029)](https://www.mayorsforpeace.org)**
  A coordinated civic action framework centered around elevating the **V**oices of hibakusha, conducting **O**utreach for TPNW support, driving **I**nternational advocacy, promoting **C**itizen action (such as petition drives), and **E**ncouraging policy transformation across thousands of cities.

* **[Global Zero](https://www.globalzero.org)**
  An international campaign launched by world leaders and security experts that advocates for a phased, verified elimination of all nuclear weapons worldwide, shifting strategic doctrines away from nuclear deterrence toward common security alternatives.

## Legislative & Municipal Campaigns

* **[ICAN Cities Appeal](https://cities.icanw.org)**
  A sister civic initiative to *Mayors for Peace*, empowering local councils, municipal governments, and mayors worldwide to formally endorse the TPNW and pressure their national governments to sign and ratify the treaty.

* **[ICAN Parliamentary Pledge](https://pledge.icanw.org)**
  A cross-party legislative campaign where parliamentarians and lawmakers in both nuclear-armed and non-nuclear states publicly commit to working toward their nation’s signature and ratification of the TPNW.

* **[Stockholm Initiative for Nuclear Disarmament](https://www.government.se/government-policy/stockholm-initiative-for-nuclear-disarmament/)**
  A government-level diplomatic initiative launched by non-nuclear states focused on "stepping stones"—actionable, near-term measures to reduce nuclear risks, strengthen compliance with the NPT, and unfreeze stalled multilateral disarmament negotiations.

## Divestment & Financial Action

* **[Defund the Warhead / Divestment Actions (PAX & Partners)](https://paxforpeace.nl)**
  Civic divestment drives targeting university endowments, municipal pension systems, and commercial banks to cease funding corporations contracted in the manufacture, maintenance, and modernization of nuclear warheads.

## University & Campus-Level Campaigns

* **[Student / Young Pugwash (ISYP / National Chapters)](https://isyp.org)**
  An international network of university students and early-career researchers spun out of the Nobel-winning Pugwash Conferences. They organize university seminars, policy hackathons, and cross-disciplinary debates on reducing technological, cyber, and nuclear existential risks.

* **"Schools of Mass Destruction" (Campus Divestment Campaign)**
  An ICAN-affiliated grassroots collegiate campaign exposing university ties to the nuclear weapons complex. It mobilizes university students and faculty to demand that their institutions terminate contracts and research partnerships with laboratories and corporations involved in nuclear warhead design and modernization.

* **[Emerging Voices Network (EVN by BASIC)](https://basicint.org/emerging-voices-network/)**
  A global collegiate and early-career digital policy network focused on diversifying who gets to speak in nuclear security spaces. It connects undergraduate and postgraduate researchers worldwide to draft collaborative policy briefs and rethink traditional deterrence theory.

## Academic Research Centers

* **[Alva Myrdal Centre for Nuclear Disarmament (Uppsala University)](https://www.uu.se/en/centre/alva-myrdal-centre)**
  An academic hub established in Sweden that combines university research, interdisciplinary working groups, and student education on irreversibility, verification technologies, and non-proliferation law.

* **[Research Center for Nuclear Weapons Abolition (RECNA, Nagasaki University)](https://www.recna.nagasaki-u.ac.jp)**
  An academic institute dedicated to nuclear abolition research and education, collaborating directly with municipal initiatives to develop university-level peace study curricula.
