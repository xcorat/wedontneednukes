**"Schools of Mass Destruction"** is a grassroots collegiate campaign coordinated by student groups and affiliated with the International Campaign to Abolish Nuclear Weapons (ICAN). It investigates and publicly exposes the deep institutional links between major higher education institutions and laboratories contracted to develop, test, and modernize nuclear warheads.

## Campaign Focus Areas

1. **Management of Nuclear Weapons Laboratories**: Calling out prominent universities (such as the University of California system and Johns Hopkins University) that hold management contracts or partnerships with national nuclear weapons laboratories like Los Alamos, Lawrence Livermore, and Sandia.
2. **Campus Recruitment Boycotts**: Organizing student walkouts and informational pickets at university career fairs when aerospace defense contractors manufacturing nuclear delivery systems attempt to recruit engineering and STEM graduates.
3. **Endowment Transparency**: Demanding full disclosure of university endowment holdings and binding divestment from corporations listed in the *Don’t Bank on the Bomb* index.

## Campus Impact

The campaign has successfully spurred student government referendums, divestment resolutions, and academic ethics panels across campuses in the United States and the United Kingdom, educating students that university prestige should not be built on the development of genocidal weapons.
