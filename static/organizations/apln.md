The **Asia-Pacific Leadership Network for Nuclear Non-Proliferation and Disarmament (APLN)** is a network of more than 150 former prime ministers, foreign and defense ministers, military chiefs, senior diplomats, and prominent scholars across 22 countries in the Asia-Pacific region.

## Regional Challenges & Focus

The Asia-Pacific region houses three declared nuclear-armed states (China, India, Pakistan), one de facto nuclear-armed state (North Korea), and major US nuclear-umbrella allies (Japan, South Korea, Australia). APLN actively works to:
- Prevent nuclear escalation in regional flashpoints: the Korean Peninsula, the Taiwan Strait, the South China Sea, and the Line of Actual Control between India and Pakistan.
- Promote institutional dialogue between Beijing, Washington, and regional capitals to avoid catastrophic miscalculation.
- Champion Nuclear-Weapon-Free Zones (such as the Southeast Asian SEANWFZ / Bangkok Treaty) and explore regional verification mechanisms.
