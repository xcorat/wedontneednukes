The **Campaign for Nuclear Disarmament (CND)** is one of Europe’s oldest and most influential grassroots peace organizations. Formed in London in 1958 following massive public outcry against thermonuclear bomb testing, CND campaigns non-violently to rid the United Kingdom and the world of nuclear weapons and other weapons of mass destruction.

In 1958, professional artist Gerald Holtom designed the internationally recognized **peace symbol** specifically for CND’s first mass demonstration march from London to the Atomic Weapons Research Establishment at Aldermaston.

## Core Campaigns

- **Scrap Trident**: Demanding the complete cancellation and decommissioning of the UK’s submarine-launched ballistic missile system (Trident and its successor Dreadnought class), redirecting tens of billions of pounds into public healthcare, education, and renewable energy.
- **Opposing NATO Nuclear Sharing**: Campaigning against the deployment and stationing of US tactical nuclear gravity bombs (such as the B61-12) at RAF Lakenheath in the UK and across European bases.
- **Supporting the TPNW**: Urging the British government to participate constructively in TPNW meetings and acknowledge the humanitarian imperative for disarmament.
