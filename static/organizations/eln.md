The **European Leadership Network (ELN)** is an independent, non-partisan network of over 300 former prime ministers, foreign ministers, defense chiefs, and parliamentarians from across Wider Europe (including NATO member states, non-aligned European nations, and Russia).

## Mission & Track-II Dialogue

ELN's primary focus is preserving strategic stability and preventing direct military conflict across the European continent:
- **Incident Prevention**: Formulating bilateral and multilateral military-to-military protocols to deconflict close encounters between NATO and Russian military aircraft, warships, and submarines.
- **Arms Control Architecture**: Analyzing the collapse of key Cold War treaties (such as the INF Treaty and New START) and drafting successor frameworks for European strategic security.
- **Nuclear Escalation Management**: Providing non-nuclear security alternatives for European defense while highlighting the existential dangers of tactical nuclear weapons deployed on European soil.
