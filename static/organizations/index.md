The global movement to eliminate nuclear weapons relies on a diverse, multilateral coalition encompassing grassroots civil society coalitions, municipal alliances, parliamentary networks, youth-led initiatives, and financial divestment efforts.

By reframing the nuclear debate from strategic deterrence to the catastrophic humanitarian, environmental, and planetary consequences of detonation, these organizations actively mobilize citizen action, champion survivor testimonies (*hibakusha*), and advocate for universal adherence to international frameworks like the [Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons (TPNW)](https://www.icanw.org/the_treaty).

---

## Featured Global Coalitions

* **[ICAN (International Campaign to Abolish Nuclear Weapons)](https://www.icanw.org)**
  A global civil society coalition made up of hundreds of partner organizations in over 100 countries. Awarded the 2017 Nobel Peace Prize, ICAN was instrumental in mobilizing public support and driving the diplomatic negotiations that brought about the UN Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons (TPNW).

* **[Mayors for Peace](https://www.mayorsforpeace.org)**
  An international municipal network established in 1982 by the cities of Hiroshima and Nagasaki that connects approximately 8,500 member cities globally. It leverages municipal leadership to elevate citizens' voices, pass down hibakusha testimonies, organize poster exhibitions, and conduct global petition drives urging states to abolish nuclear arsenals.

* **[IPPNW (International Physicians for the Prevention of Nuclear War)](https://www.ippnw.org)**
  A federation of medical and public health professionals (awarded the 1985 Nobel Peace Prize) that originally proposed launching ICAN. IPPNW focuses on educating policymakers and the public on the catastrophic medical, environmental, and humanitarian consequences of nuclear detonations.

* **[Youth for TPNW](https://www.youth4tpnw.org)**
  A transnational network run by and for young people aged 18 to 30. The organization focuses on youth mobilization, centering the lived experiences of affected communities and nuclear survivors, and bridging disarmament advocacy with intersectional social justice and climate action.

* **[PNND (Parliamentarians for Nuclear Non-proliferation and Disarmament)](https://www.pnnd.org)**
  A non-partisan, cross-border network for national and international parliamentarians. It empowers legislators to introduce parliamentary resolutions, scrutinize state budgets, question national security policies, and build political will for disarmament frameworks.

* **[Peace Boat](https://peaceboat.org)**
  An international NGO and ICAN International Steering Group partner that travels globally aboard a passenger ship to promote peace, human rights, and nuclear abolition, frequently touring with hibakusha to share first-hand accounts of nuclear devastation.

* **[ICRC (International Committee of the Red Cross)](https://www.icrc.org)**
  An impartial humanitarian organization that plays a pivotal role in disarmament advocacy by establishing the fundamental incompatibility of nuclear weapons with International Humanitarian Law (IHL) and highlighting the lack of adequate humanitarian response capacity in the event of a detonation.

## Survivor Advocacy & Grassroots Mass Movements

* **[Nihon Hidankyo (Japan Confederation of A- and H-Bomb Sufferers Organizations)](https://www.ne.jp/asahi/hidankyo/nihon/)**
  The nationwide confederation of atomic bomb survivors (hibakusha) from Hiroshima and Nagasaki. Awarded the 2024 Nobel Peace Prize, Hidankyo has spent decades providing firsthand witness testimonies to establish a moral taboo against the use and possession of nuclear arms.

* **[CND (Campaign for Nuclear Disarmament)](https://cnduk.org)**
  One of Europe’s longest-running grassroots mass-movement campaigns (originators of the iconic peace symbol). CND mobilizes public opposition in the UK and internationally against nuclear weapons modernization (such as Trident) and NATO nuclear sharing.

* **[Abolition 2000](https://www.abolition2000.org)**
  A global civil society network bringing together hundreds of organizations across more than 90 countries. It coordinates international civil society working groups focused on a binding Nuclear Weapons Convention, missile defense risks, and redirecting nuclear weapons budgets toward social and environmental needs.

* **[Gensuikin (Japan Congress Against A- and H-Bombs)](https://www.gensuikin.org)**
  One of Japan’s leading citizen-led mass movements founded in the wake of the 1954 Bikini Atoll nuclear test. Gensuikin organizes annual world conferences, supports radiation victims worldwide, and campaigns against both nuclear armament and commercial nuclear power risks.

## Scientific Diplomacy & Policy Think Tanks

* **[Pugwash Conferences on Science and World Affairs](https://pugwash.org)**
  An international organization founded in 1957 following the Russell-Einstein Manifesto, and recipient of the 1995 Nobel Peace Prize. Pugwash brings together scientists and public figures to reduce the danger of armed conflict and develop technical solutions for nuclear threat reduction and verifiable arms control.

* **[BASIC (British American Security Information Council)](https://basicint.org)**
  An independent think-tank and track-II diplomatic organization that fosters cross-bloc dialogue on nuclear risk reduction, responsibilities of nuclear-armed states under the NPT, and youth inclusion through its *Emerging Voices Network (EVN)*.

* **[Nuclear Threat Initiative (NTI)](https://www.nti.org)**
  A non-partisan global security organization focused on preventing catastrophic attacks with weapons of mass destruction. NTI develops practical diplomatic models, public tracking indexes, and technical verification frameworks to build political paths toward global zero.

* **[UNIDIR (United Nations Institute for Disarmament Research)](https://unidir.org)**
  An autonomous, voluntarily funded research institute based in Geneva within the UN system. It produces independent, evidence-based research on nuclear arms control, verification technologies, and emerging risks to inform UN diplomatic processes and member state policy.

## Regional Networks & Grassroots Coalitions

* **[Asia-Pacific Leadership Network (APLN)](https://www.apln.network)**
  A regional security network of former political leaders, diplomats, and scholars across the Asia-Pacific region. APLN advocates for tension reduction, dialogue between regional nuclear powers, and expanding Nuclear-Weapon-Free Zones (NWFZs) in Northeast Asia.

* **[European Leadership Network (ELN)](https://www.europeanleadershipnetwork.org)**
  A pan-European network of former heads of state, defense ministers, and policy experts. ELN works to prevent miscalculation between NATO and Russia, preserve what remains of strategic arms control frameworks, and propose non-nuclear security alternatives for Europe.

* **[OPANAL (Agency for the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons in Latin America and the Caribbean)](https://www.opanal.org)**
  The intergovernmental organization established by the pioneering Treaty of Tlatelolco, which created the world's first nuclear-weapon-free zone in a densely populated region. OPANAL works across Latin America to ensure strict non-proliferation compliance and advocates for nuclear disarmament worldwide.

* **[Middle East Treaty Organization (METO)](https://www.middleeasttreatyorganization.org)**
  A regional civil society initiative dedicated to advancing a Middle East free of nuclear weapons and other weapons of mass destruction (WMDFZ). METO brings together regional activists and experts to build draft treaties, facilitate track-II diplomatic discussions, and cultivate civil society support across the region.

* **[Nuclear Free Local Authorities (NFLA – UK & Ireland)](https://www.nuclearfreeplanet.org)**
  A longstanding network of local government councils across the UK and Ireland working alongside groups like CND. NFLA coordinates municipal resolutions, opposes the transportation of nuclear warheads through local districts, and pushes for the phase-out of the UK's Trident submarine program.
