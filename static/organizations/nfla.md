**Nuclear Free Local Authorities (NFLA)** is a municipal association founded in Manchester in 1980 that represents local government councils across England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland, and the Republic of Ireland.

NFLA provides local authorities with independent research, policy guidance, and collaborative strategies on civil nuclear energy hazards, radioactive waste transport, and the humanitarian risks of nuclear weapons.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Trident Warhead Transport Tracking**: Monitoring and exposing the road transport of nuclear warheads along public motorways and through densely populated towns between the Atomic Weapons Establishment (AWE) in Berkshire and the naval base at Faslane, Scotland.
- **Municipal Emergency Preparedness**: Advising local councils on the profound insufficiency of civil defense plans in the event of radiation leaks, nuclear transport accidents, or nuclear detonation.
- **Support for Mayors for Peace & TPNW**: Serving as the UK and Ireland chapter secretariat for *Mayors for Peace*, passing formal council resolutions supporting the UN Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons.
