**Peace Boat** is an international non-governmental organization based in Japan that holds Special Consultative Status with the United Nations Economic and Social Council (ECOSOC). Established in 1983, Peace Boat operates chartered passenger ships that circumnavigate the globe on three-to-four month educational peace voyages.

Peace Boat serves on the International Steering Group of the International Campaign to Abolish Nuclear Weapons (ICAN).

## The Hibakusha Project

Since 2008, Peace Boat has coordinated the **Global Voyage for a Nuclear-Free World: Peace Boat Hibakusha Project**, inviting atomic bomb survivors from Hiroshima and Nagasaki as well as indigenous survivors of global nuclear testing to join voyages around the world.

During ports of call in over 80 countries, hibakusha participate in town hall sessions, university lectures, and high-level meetings with government ministers, heads of state, and media to convey the human reality of nuclear catastrophe.
