The **Research Center for Nuclear Weapons Abolition (RECNA)** is an academic think-tank and research institute established in April 2012 at Nagasaki University—the only university in the world situated in a city attacked with a plutonium atomic bomb.

RECNA bridges Nagasaki’s historical survivor legacy with modern, policy-relevant strategic analysis.

## Key Research Programs

- **Northeast Asia Nuclear-Weapon-Free Zone (NEA-NWFZ)**: Designing comprehensive diplomatic models to de-escalate tensions on the Korean Peninsula and achieve mutual, verified non-nuclear commitments among Japan, South Korea, and North Korea, with negative security assurances from nuclear powers.
- **Global Nuclear Warhead Tracking Index**: Publishing an annual, interactive global map detailing every known nuclear warhead across the nine nuclear-armed states, tracking modernization trends and alert postures.
- **Peace & Disarmament Curricula**: Collaborating with municipal initiatives like Mayors for Peace to develop university courses and public lecture series analyzing the reality of nuclear weapons.
