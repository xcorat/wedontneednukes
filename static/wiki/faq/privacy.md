## What we collect

- **Anonymous votes** — a single, rotating `anon_id` cookie separate
  from any account. Used only to deduplicate your answer if you return.
- **Account data** — if you sign in, we keep your email and a hashed
  device sessions list. Nothing else.
- **Bot defense** — Cloudflare Turnstile runs in the background and
  does not track you across sites.

## What we never do

- We never sell data.
- We never build advertising profiles.
- We never link your anonymous vote to your real-world identity
  without an explicit "claim vote" action you take yourself.
