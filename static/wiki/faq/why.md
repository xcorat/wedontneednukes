## 12,000+ Warheads

Nine nations possess over 12,000 nuclear weapons. An accidental launch,
miscalculation, or escalation risks human civilization. The math on
mutually assured destruction has not changed — but the number of states
with fingers on the trigger has grown.

## Consensus Before Politics

Complex treaties and negotiations stall. But agreeing on the fundamental
truth that humanity does not need tools of total mutual destruction is
where real change starts. Politics follows consensus, not the other way
around.

## Your Voice Shapes The Movement

Whether you fully agree or have reservations, every voice helps measure
global consensus and guide grassroots action. The premise is the
starting point — the pledge is the action that follows.
